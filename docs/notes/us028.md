# Story [[camera]] [[product]]

## role
[[pn001|developer]]

## want
the ultrawide camera to focus by itself on ordinary scenes, with a focus
position camera software can set

## because
its lens rests at close focus whenever its focus motor is unpowered, so
every still comes out soft ([[poc028]], [[us027]])

## acceptance_criteria
- after a reboot, opening the ultrawide through libcamera exposes a
  controllable lens (a LensPosition control), with no hand-run power or
  I2C steps
- by default, a still of a distant lit scene is sharp; it is visibly
  sharper than the same scene with the motor unpowered
- setting the lens position through the camera API moves focus between
  near and far subjects, visible in stills
- when no app uses the camera, the focus motor is not powered
- the front camera and full-resolution stills ([[us026]]) still work
