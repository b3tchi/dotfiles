# Story [[camera]] [[product]]

## role
[[pn001|developer]]

## want
to adjust the ultrawide's focus and exposure from the keyboard while the
camera overlay is open

## because
the lens and manual exposure can now be driven ([[us028]], [[poc028]]), but
only through ad-hoc test scripts; the overlay always shoots at the default
focus and auto-exposure

## acceptance_criteria
- in the camera layer, keys move focus nearer / farther in steps and back
  to the default far focus; the preview follows within a frame or two
- in the camera layer, keys switch exposure between auto and manual and
  make the picture brighter / darker in steps; the preview follows
- the current focus and exposure setting is visible in the overlay
- leaving the camera layer or closing the overlay leaves nothing stuck:
  the next camera start uses the default focus and auto-exposure
- stills taken with the overlay use the focus and exposure shown
