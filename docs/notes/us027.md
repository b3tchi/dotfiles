# Story [[camera]] [[product]]

## role
[[pn001]]

## want
to find out whether the phone's ultrawide camera module (s5kjn1) has a
focus actuator the kernel can drive, and if so to move its lens

## because
stills come out soft: the lens sits parked near close focus ([[poc027]])
and libcamera exposes no Af / LensPosition controls, because no lens
driver or lens-focus link exists ([[us026]])

## acceptance_criteria
- whether the s5kjn1 module has a focus actuator (VCM) is established:
  chip name, CCI/I2C bus and address, backed by evidence (i2cdetect on
  the phone's CCI bus, downstream/LineageOS device tree or a vendor dump)
- if a VCM exists: the lens is moved from userspace on the phone (v4l2
  subdev FOCUS_ABSOLUTE or a raw I2C write), and a still of a distant
  scene is visibly sharper than today's soft one (side-by-side crop)
- if no VCM exists: "fixed-focus module" is recorded as a known
  limitation in [[ft019]] and the soft-focus question is closed
- findings land in a poc### zettel; when a VCM is confirmed, a follow-up
  story (e.g. kernel driver / DT node + lens-focus link) is drafted
