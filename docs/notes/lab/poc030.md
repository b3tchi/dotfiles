# PoC [[cat002]] [[board]]

## informs
[[sp045|us030-camera-keyboard-focus-exposure]]

## hypothesis
The process that streams the s5kjn1 ultrawide through libcamera can also
drive the lens V4L2 subdev ([[sp044]]: `dw9719 18-000c`,
`focus_absolute`) while streaming, from its own fd, and the picture
follows within a frame or two. Manual `AeEnable` / `ExposureTime` /
`AnalogueGain` set on requests take effect live and come back in request
metadata, and AE-on metadata carries exposure time and gain (to seed
manual mode).

## method
Throwaway C++ spike (`poc/focus-stream` worktree of libcamera-qml,
`tests/poc_focus_stream.cpp`, discarded), libcamera 0.8 on the phone,
kernel 7.2.9, 2026-11-23 13:01. One process: CameraManager, first back
camera (`camera@2d`), 1280x720 ABGR8888 viewfinder as `CameraSource`.
Lens found generically: the first `/dev/v4l-subdev*` answering
`VIDIOC_QUERYCTRL(V4L2_CID_FOCUS_ABSOLUTE)` (`/dev/v4l-subdev28`, 0-1023,
default 0), opened AFTER the stream started (frame 30). Frame-indexed
script: focus 385 / 600 / 385 at frames 45 / 75 / 105 (S_CTRL then
G_CTRL), then manual exposure 10 / 20 / 10 ms at gain 2.0 from frame
140 / 170 / 200 on every requeued request. Per frame: Laplacian
variance and mean luma of a 256x256 centre crop, metadata; crops saved
as PNG around each change. Negative control: the same focus writes with
the stream stopped. Scene per the camera test protocol (bd
dotfiles-dqhj): phone upright in a stand, printed page at 9 cm, desk
lamp on the left.

## result
- Focus while streaming: validated. Every `VIDIOC_S_CTRL` returned 0 and
  `VIDIOC_G_CTRL` read back the written value (385, 600, 385), with
  libcamera holding its own lens fd. The picture changed 1 frame after
  each write (crop luma 218.9 -> 216.7 at frame 76, 215.1 -> 216.6 at
  106; blur and a bokeh disc visibly change in the crops). Writes with
  the stream stopped behave the same.
- Sharpness: NOT measured. AE sat at its limits (33152 us, gain 64) and
  the crop was blown out with lamp glare; the page was not in the crop,
  so the Laplacian moved ~4% and proves nothing. Moved to [[sp045]]
  Task 4's on-device test (user decision 2026-11-23).
- Manual exposure: validated. Metadata echoes `ExposureTime` 9994 /
  19995 and `AnalogueGain` 2.0 3-4 frames after the control is set; the
  image follows 7-9 frames after it is set (~230-300 ms; set at 139 /
  170 / 200, image at 148 / 177 / 207). 10 -> 20 ms raised crop luma
  27.0 -> 41.5 (sRGB, ~2x linear). The two 10 ms phases differ by 11%
  (27.0 vs 24.0), over the protocol's 5% drift limit, so the luma ratios
  are not evidence; that the controls apply is.
- AE-on metadata carries `ExposureTime` and `AnalogueGain` (seeding
  manual mode works); no `AeEnable` and no `LensPosition` in metadata.
- ControlInfo: `ExposureTime` [62..33152] us, `AnalogueGain` [1..64],
  `FrameDurationLimits` [33323..509300] us, `AeEnable` [false..true].
- The spike aborted in its own teardown after the result
  (`std::system_error: Operation not permitted`, static destruction
  order); not related to the question.

## recommendation
Go ahead with [[sp045]] as written: `CameraSource` opens the lens subdev
itself (generic QUERYCTRL lookup) and writes `focus_absolute` on the GUI
thread while streaming; manual exposure as request controls on every
requeued request, seeded from AE metadata. Expect ~7-9 frames from an
exposure step to the picture; focus is immediate. Task 4 must measure
sharpness with a usable scene: manual exposure during the focus check,
lamp out of view, page at the crop centre, and a full frame saved to
check framing. Task 5 should bound exposure steps by `ExposureTime`
[62..33152] and `AnalogueGain` [1..64].
