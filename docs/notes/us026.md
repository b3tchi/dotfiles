## acceptance_criteria
- `capture(path)` on the phone's back camera writes an upright JPEG of
  2880x3840 (3840x2880 rotated: the largest still size below the sensor
  mode; 4080x3072 1:1 output is corrupted by the software ISP, [[poc027]],
  user decision 2026-11-22)
- the preview pauses during the still (the `simple` pipeline gives one
  stream only): the last preview frame stays on screen with a
  "capturing…" indicator (new read-only `capturing` property) and
  streaming resumes by itself afterwards; tests/smoke.qml still passes
  (>= 15 fps) after the resume
- `capture()` to `captured()` takes at most 6 s on the phone
- a camera with no still size larger than the preview falls back to the
  preview frame and says so in `error`
- autofocus is out of scope: the s5kjn1 has no lens driver / lens-focus
  link and libcamera exposes no Af controls (known limitation)
