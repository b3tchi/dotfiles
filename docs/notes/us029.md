# Story [[camera]] [[product]]

## role
[[pn001|developer]]

## want
the ultrawide camera's auto-exposure to follow exposure compensation
(EV) and to keep manual exposure working, measured under controlled,
repeatable conditions

## because
without a sensor helper the software ISP ignores EV; with the simple
helper tried in [[sp044]] the AE pins at its limits and manual exposure
goes black (digital gain 1e+08), and the earlier measurements were taken
without a prepared scene ([[us028]], [[poc029]])

## acceptance_criteria
- every camera measurement follows the written test protocol: phone fixed
  in a stand, a defined target at a set distance, fixed lighting with
  daylight excluded, conditions recorded with each result, both variants
  compared within the same run, reference frames saved for review
- with auto-exposure on, EV -1 / +1 roughly halve / double the measured
  brightness of the reference scene (within 25%), and EV -2 brings a
  scene auto-exposure blows out back to a readable image
- manual exposure time and gain still work live, with no runaway digital
  gain
- the ultrawide's auto-exposure on the reference scenes is no worse than
  today's (judged on the saved reference frames)
